# Lendable

## Definition

An app that lets employees borrow shared company equipment and lets the
equipment manager approve and track it without a spreadsheet.

## Users and situations

- Employees reach for it when they need a camera, laptop, or projector for a
  shoot, trip, or event.
- The office equipment manager uses it to approve requests and confirm returns.

## Problem and current alternatives

Employees ask the manager in chat, and the manager tracks who holds what in a
shared spreadsheet that drifts out of date.

## Promised change

Anyone can see what is available and get it, and the manager always knows who
holds each item.

## Core loop

An employee searches for equipment, requests a loan for dates, the manager
approves it, and the manager confirms the return.

## Capabilities and boundaries

- Only employees of the company can borrow.
- Purchasing, repairs, and asset depreciation stay outside the product.

## Experience principles

- Availability shown to an employee must never be stale.

## Success signals

- Fewer loans become overdue by more than three days.

## Assumptions and unknowns

- Unknown: whether loans should ever need a second approver.
