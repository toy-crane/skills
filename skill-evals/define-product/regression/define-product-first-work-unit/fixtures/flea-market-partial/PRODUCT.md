# Booth

## Definition

An app that lets small flea-market organizers fill their booths with sellers
without juggling forms, transfers, and chat threads.

## Users and situations

- Organizers of monthly neighborhood flea markets with 20–60 booths use it
  while recruiting sellers for the next market.
- Hobby sellers use it when they want a booth at a market they follow.

## Problem and current alternatives

Organizers collect applications through a form, check bank transfers by hand,
and send booth numbers one by one in chat.

## Promised change

An organizer knows at any moment which booths are confirmed, and a seller knows
where to set up without asking.

## Core loop

The organizer opens a market, sellers apply for a booth, the organizer accepts
applicants, accepted sellers pay the booth fee, and each paid seller receives a
booth assignment.

## Capabilities and boundaries

- Organizers run their own markets; the app is not a market directory.
- Sales between sellers and visitors stay outside the product.

## Experience principles

- A seller's current status must be readable in one glance.

## Success signals

- Fewer booths stay empty on market day.

## Assumptions and unknowns

- Unknown: whether organizers want refunds handled in the app.
